What the report delivers:
The findings in seven chapters

The transformation of the economy is necessary – and already underway. Transparency regarding transformation performance has so far not kept pace. Now Transforming Performance shows for the first time, based on robust data, how private equity and venture capital firms position themselves on transformation and how to recognise firms with pronounced transformation competence.
